How SETU works

A four-stage pipeline, each stage backed by its own module.

01

AI matching & risk prediction

The problem statement is read by the matching engine, which ranks registered startups by skill-fit and pairs each match with a live risk score — track record, profile strength, and compliance standing, not a black box.

02

Verified startup database

Every startup keeps a public profile — specialisation, achievements, DPIIT status, verification badge — that both the matching and scoring engines read from directly.

03

Transparent scoring & leaderboard

A weighted, explainable formula (policy fit, profile strength, past feedback) ranks every startup on a public leaderboard — no hidden criteria.

04

Finance tracking & feedback loop

Once a startup is selected, a 4-milestone contract tracks scope and funds through to payment. Feedback after completion flows straight back into that startup's score for the next match.

Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible to register as a startup?

Any DPIIT-recognised startup, or one in the process of recognition, may register. DPIIT recognition removes the usual prior-turnover and past-experience requirements for pilot-stage engagements.

How is the match score calculated?

The matching engine compares the skills a problem statement needs against each startup's declared tags. It's a transparent overlap score, not a hidden model — you can see exactly which terms matched.

What happens if a milestone is missed?

Funds for a milestone are only released once it is marked delivered and verified. A missed milestone simply withholds that tranche — it does not cancel earlier approved payments.

Is my startup's data secure?

Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text, and every session uses a unique, expiring access token. See the Feedback and Finance pages for how pilot outcomes are recorded transparently.
